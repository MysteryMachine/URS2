# Backgrounds, Experiences, and Bonds

## Backgrounds and Experiences

Backgrounds and Experiences comprise the core character progression and identity mechanics of URS. Both provide Stats, Skills, Items, and other roleplaying flavor to your characters. The only difference between a Background and an Experience mechanically, is that Backgrounds cannot be taken after the start of the game. Here is an example Background.

## Tracks

URS is a a game with free-form character building, but we recognize that, especially for newer players, it is valuable to provide some rails to player progression. For this purpose, we've provided a couple of tracks for players to follow to learn how to build characters to function in a specific way.

We'll be providing three types of tracks, from easiest to play, to hardest.

* **Combat**
* **Support**
* **Magic**

### Combat Tracks

The core Skills to build around for combat are Duel and Volley, for melee and ranged combat respectively. With Duel, you are able to automatically hit less skilled creatures
