# Character

Players are mostly responsible for building and developing their character. 

## Stats

URS has 5 stats, Power, Precision, Constitution, Wit, and Willpower. For humans, without magical aid, these stats will range from -2 to 2, where 0 represents average human ability. A person with a score of 2 is someone within the top 20% of all humans, and conversely, someone with a score of -2 is within the bottom 20% of all humans.

Humans are not capable of achieving stats higher than +2 without magical aid. If someone gains a **Natural** stat boost, it can only increase that stat to +2. A **Supernatural** boost will allow a human to improve their status above +2. Individuals with a +3 or better in a stat are said to have a supernatural aptitude towards that stat, e.g., supernatural Power or supernatural Wit.

* **Power (POW)** represents an individual's ability to generate physical force and speed. Individuals with a high Power score are able to sprint quickly, lift heavy weights, and jump high.
* **Precision (PRE)** represents an individual's dexterity and balance. Individuals with a high Precision score are able to manipulate their body precisely, manipulate objects dextrously, and balance themselves.
* **Constitution (CON)** represents the hardiness of an individual's body. Individuals with a high Constituion score will find themselves able to run longer distances, resist poison and disease, and take more of a beating before going down.
* **Wit (WIT)** represents an individuals ability to learn and apply their knowledge. Individuals with a high Wit score will find themselves being able to learn from books, have a better ability to persuade others and make friends, and be capbale of understanding the world better.
* **Willpower (WIL)** represents an individuals ability to focus, and their mental fortitude. Individuals with a high Wit will find themselves being able to better percieve danger, to have a stronger and more reliable sense of intuition, and to be able to mentally weather difficult situations. Individuals with supernatural levels of Willpower will find their Willpower to be able to affect the state of reality.

In addition to your base stats, you have a few **Derived Stats** that are directly determined by your Base Stats.

* **Hit Points (HP)** are a representation of your physical health. Events that harm your body will lower your HP. Your max HP is equal to 12 plus 3 times your Constitution score. When your HP goes to 0, you gain **Comatose**.
* **Will Points (WP)** are a representation of your mental strength. Events that scare you or demoralize you will lower your WP. Your max WP is equal to 12 plus 3 times your Willpower score. When your WP goes to 0, you gain **Mental Break**.
* **Carry Capacity** is a measure of how many kilograms you can carry around without being encumbered. You are able to carry 12 plus 3 times your Power score in kilograms of equipment and items.
* **Speed** is a measure, in meters, of how far you can move in a turn unencumbered. It is equal to 8 plus your Power score.

## Items

Items are things like coins, weapons, tools, or clothing that your character obtains. A copper coin represents about a day's manual labor.

### Equipment

URS aims to keep equipment simple. You have your main Armor, the things in your hands, and anything else you wear is an Accessory. 

* **Armor** is your whole body armor, and is used to determine your defence. Armor has a **Defence Score**. When taking damage, reduce it by your armor's Defence Score.
* **Hands** are what you are wielding. You are able to hold two 1-handed items or one 2-handed item. If you are wielding a weapon, it will have an associated Damage Dice and a Reach. A weapon's **Damage Dice** is the dice you roll when dealing damage with a weapon. For fists or improvised weapons, this roll is usually 1d3, unless otherwise indicated by your Storyteller. A weapon's **Reach** is a number, in meters, indicating how far a weapon can reach. For improvised weapons and fists, this is usually 1 meter unless indicated by your Storyteller.
* **Accessories** are any additional items you are wearing. You are able to equip as many accessories as you like, provided it makes physical sense for you to do so. Accessories can replace parts of your armor, e.g., magical helmets, gloves, boots.

Equipment may require certain Stats or a Skill to use properly. Generally, mundane melee weapons do not have requirements. Bows and other ranged weapons can be used by those without training by Improvising, but otherwise require a Skill to use easily. Magical or supernatural weapons might have Stat or Experience requirements to be used.

## Background, Experiences, and Skills

**Skills** are the rules that determine how your character interacts with the world. All characters start with four Skills, Improvise, Slay, Rest & Prepare, and Travel.

All characters start with one **Background**. Your Background will provide a base to roleplay off, as well as starting Stats, Skills, and Items.

An **Experience** can be gained in many ways. Many Experiences have specific conditions required in order to be able to be taken. Experiences, like Backgrounds, provide additional Stats, Skills, and Items.

## Character Creation

1. **Prepare your character sheet.** Go to the Character Sheet and create a new character. All stats start at a score of -2.
2. **Select your Background.** Apply any stat points to your sheet as indicated by your Background. Add any items indicated by your background to your character sheet.
3. **Select any starting Experiences.** Your storyteller will you how many, and which types of Experiences you're allowed to select. It is recommended your characters start with 1 to 3 Basic Experiences, with less experiences being appropriate for newer players.
4. **Distrbute your additional stat points.** You have 8 additonal points to distribute freely, up to a max of +2.
5. **Calculate your derived stats.** If you're using the online character sheet, it will calculate this for you. Otherwise, follow the instructions in the **Derived Stats** section.

*Hint: In URS, you will find yourself regularly using all your stats. It is recommended beginners do not overly focus on specializing.*
