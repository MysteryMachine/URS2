# Character

## Stats

### Base Stats

URS has 5 stats, Power (POW), Precision (PRE), Constituion (CON), Wit (WIT), and Willpower (WIL). For humans, without magical aid, these stats will range from -2 to 2, where 0 represents average human ability. A person with a score of 2 is someone within the top 20% of all humans, and conversely, someone with a score of -2 is within the bottom 20% of all humans.

Humans are not capable of achieving stats higher than +2 without magical aid. If someone gains a **Natural** stat boost, it can only increase that stat to +2. A **Supernatural** boost will allow a human to improve their status above +2. Individuals with a +3 or better in a stat are said to have a supernatural aptitude towards that stat, e.g., supernatural Power or supernatural Wit.

#### Power
Power represents an individual's ability to generate physical force and speed. Individuals with a high Power score are able to sprint quickly, lift heavy weights, and jump high.

#### Precision
Precision represents an individual's dexterity and balance. Individuals with a high Precision score are able to manipulate their body precisely, manipulate objects dextrously, and balance themselves.

#### Constitution
Constitution represents the hardiness of an individual's body. Individuals with a high Constituion score will find themselves able to run longer distances, resist poison and disease, and take more of a beating before going down.

#### Wit
Wit represents an individuals ability to learn and apply their knowledge. Individuals with a high Wit score will find themselves being able to learn from books, have a better ability to persuade others and make friends, and be capbale of understanding the world better.

#### Willpower
Willpower represents an individuals ability to focus, and their mental fortitude. Individuals with a high Wit will find themselves being able to better percieve danger, to have a stronger and more reliable sense of intuition, and to be able to mentally weather difficult situations.

Individuals with supernatural levels of Willpower will find their Willpower to be able to affect the state of reality.

### Derived Stats
Derived Stats are directly directly determined by your Base Stats.

#### Hit Points (HP)
Your Hit Points are a representation of your physical health. Events that harm your body will lower your HP. Your max HP is equal to 12 + 3 times your Constitution. When your HP goes to 0, you gain **Comatose**.

#### Will Points (WP)
Your Will Points are a representation of your mental strength. Events that scare you or demoralize you will lower your WP. Your max WP is equal to 12 + 3 times your Willpower. When your WP goes to 0, you gain **Mental Break**.

#### Carry Capacity
You are able to carry 12 + 3 times your Power kilograms of equipment and items.

#### Defence
Defence is a flat reduction to damage taken, provided by your armor.

#### Reach
Reach determines how close you have to get to a target to be able to attack them with a weapon. It is derived from your weapon, and is a number, in meters.

#### Damage Dice
Weapons have a damage dice associated with them. When dealing damage with that weapon, you may be asked to roll the associated dice to determine damage dealt. Attacking with your fist uses a d2 as your damage dice.

## Background and Experiences

#### Background
All characters start with one Background. Your Background will provide a base to roleplay off, as well as starting Stats, Skills, and Items.

#### Experiences
An Experience can be gained in many ways. Many Experiences have specific conditions required in order to be able to be taken. Experiences, like Backgrounds, provide additional Stats, Skills, and Item.

## Items and Equipment

#### Equipment Slots

* **Armour:** This slot represents your whole body armor, and is used to determine your defence.
* **Hands:** You are able to hold two 1-handed items or one 2-handed item.
* **Accessories:** You are able to equip as many accessories as you like, provided it makes physical sense for you to do so.

## Character Creation

1. **Prepare your character sheet:** Go to the Character Sheet and create a new character. All stats start at -2.
2. **Select your Background:** Select a Background. Apply any stat points to your sheet as indicated by your Background. Add any items indicated by your background to your character sheet.
3. **Distrbute your additional stat points:**  You have 8 additonal points to distribute freely, up to a max of +2.
4. **Calculate your derived stats:** If you're using the online character sheet, it will calculate this for you. Otherwise, follow the instructions in the **Derived Stats** section.

*Hint: In URS, you will find yourself regularly using all your stats. It is recommended beginners do not overly focus on specializing.*
