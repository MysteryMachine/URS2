# URS

URS is a roleplaying system developed by Sal Becker, aiming to provide deep, free-form character development and fast, free-form roleplay without tons of slow rolls and math.

## Key Features

* A simple 5 stat system where stats directly represent their bonuses. All rolls are 2d6 plus stat bonuses.
* A Background and Experience system that allows your characters to develop in a free-form, classless way.
* Magic is rare and mysterious, requiring individuals to make special appeals to the gods, find rare artefacts, or discovery hidden knowledge to be used.
