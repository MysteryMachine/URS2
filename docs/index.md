# URS

URS is a roleplaying system developed by Sal Becker, aiming to provide deep, free-form character development and fast, free-form roleplay without tons of slow rolls and math.

## Key Features

* A simple 5 stat system where stats directly represent their bonuses. Most rolls are 2d6 plus stat bonuses.
* A Background and Experience system that allows your characters to develop in a free-form, classless way.
* Magic is rare and mysterious, requiring individuals to make special appeals to the gods, find rare artefacts, or discovery hidden knowledge to be used.

## The Storyteller and Players

A game of URS is played by Players and facilitated by a Storyteller. The Storyteller's job is to help translate the Player's roleplaying into rules. The Player is encouraged to describe their actions, and the Storyteller will help the Player determine what Skills, if any, to invoke, and what dice need to be rolled. For example, suppose a Player describes the following action.

<div style={{ marginLeft: "24px" }}>
  **Player:** *"I dash towards the creature with my sword ready, and slash at its midsection"*
</div>

The Storyteller might respond with a simple continuation.

<div style={{ marginLeft: "24px" }}>
  **Storyteller:** *"The creature, distracted by the commotion, fails to see you coming. You run it through, and it falls to the ground, dead."*
</div>

In this situation, no rolls or rules were invoked, and the roleplay continues. URS is intended to reduce the amount of dice rolls required to navigate the game, and if a dice roll would be superfluous or excessive, URS generally encourages the Storyteller to simply allow the action to continue.

Let's investigate a situation where the Storyteller might invoke a rule.

<div style={{ marginLeft: "24px" }}>
  **Player:** *"I quickly try to pick the lock before the duchess returns."*<br />
  **Storyteller:** *"You're under duress, do you have any Skills that help you pick the lock?"*<br />
  **Player:** *"No, I do not."*<br />
  **Storyteller:** *"You Improvise. Roll and add Precision."*
</div>

Players are tasked with knowing what unique Skills their character has and when they they apply. In a normal game, this will mean 2 to 4 Skills. It is the Storyteller's responsibility to adjudicate any other rulings and to determine if a dice roll is required.
